Sign in to Yoto

To connect your Yoto players to HomeKit, sign in with your Yoto account:

  1. Click Sign in with Yoto. The Yoto sign-in page opens in a new tab.
  2. Sign in and approve access.
  3. Your browser then shows a "This site can't be reached" page. That's expected.
  4. Copy the full address from that page's address bar and paste it back here.
